# Toybox Ledger Local — downloads

Toybox Ledger for your own computer: your books stay on your PC, encrypted, with no account and no sign-in.

**[Download the latest installer for Windows](https://github.com/cmascho-dev/toybox-ledger-releases/releases/latest/download/ToyboxLedgerLocal-Setup.exe)**
· [all versions](https://github.com/cmascho-dev/toybox-ledger-releases/releases)

- Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. The installer needs no administrator rights.
- It adds Toybox Ledger to the Start menu and, if you like, the desktop. It opens in your browser.
- The first time, you choose a passphrase and get a recovery key. Keep the recovery key safe: without the passphrase
  or the recovery key, nobody can open your books.
- Your books live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ToyboxLedger`, with automatic backups beside them. Installing a newer version or
  uninstalling keeps them.

These builds are not signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC". Choose **More info**, then
**Run anyway**.

This repository holds only the release files. They are built and published automatically from the Toybox Ledger
source.
